Join Us!

We are looking for talented and motivated individuals to join our team and lead the next generation of synthetic biology and cell engineering. We are looking for graduate students, postdoctoral fellows, and PhD scientists.

Postdoctoral and Clinical Fellows

Inquiries about postdoctoral position should be emailed to Wendell Lim (limpostdoc@ucsf.edu).

  • •   UCSF/Berkeley Bioengineering Program
  • •   UCSF Pharmaceutical Sciences and Pharmacogenomics
  • •   UCSF Chemistry and Chemical Biology.
  • •   UCSF Developmental & Stem Cell Biology.

Current UCSF first years should email Wendell Lim (wendell.lim[at]ucsf.edu) and CC Noleine Blizzard (noleine.blizzard[at]ucsf.edu) to express interest in joining the lab.
